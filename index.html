About Us

The kape.dev crew

We're a group of local app builders — student developers who bond over coffee and late-night coding sessions. Kape — Filipino for coffee — is more than a name. It's how we start every build.

We build apps that solve real problems, especially for people in our community. Our goal: ship clean, useful software that anyone can use.

From concept to APK to interactive web maps, everything we make is built with care, curiosity, and a whole lot of caffeine.
